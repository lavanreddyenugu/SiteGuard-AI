SAFETY PRECAUTIONS

Follow these safety precautions before entering or working at a construction site.

⛑️

Wear Safety Helmet

Always wear an approved safety helmet to protect your head from falling objects.

🦺

Wear Safety Vest

Use a high-visibility vest so workers can easily be identified.

🥾

Safety Boots

Wear protective footwear to prevent injuries caused by heavy or sharp objects.

🪢

Safety Harness

Workers operating at height should use suitable fall-protection equipment.

⚡

Electrical Safety

Keep away from exposed wires and unauthorized electrical equipment.

🔥

Fire Safety

Keep emergency exits clear and know the location of fire extinguishers.

🚧

Restricted Areas

Never enter restricted or hazardous areas without authorization.

📢

Report Hazards

Immediately report unsafe conditions, injuries or dangerous situations.
